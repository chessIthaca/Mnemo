1. Code-path proof. `assistant_age` counts ASSISTANT turns only (openai.rs:1360-1376,

   trailing tool/system messages. The policy consult is a pure (kind, model) function

   reasoning_field = "reasoning_content"). The age-0 injection (openai.rs:1443-1451) keys

   unconditionally. Therefore NO post-b23463c build_request_json can emit an unkeyed

   regardless of what is on disk; the release exe was rebuilt 04:14, 15 min AFTER the
   incident (consistent with a post-crash rebuild).
2. Regression test. `deepseek_tail_owner_keyed_when_system_follows_tool_results`
   (src/provider/openai.rs tests) encodes the exact incident shape — historical unkeyed
   fallback turn, age-0 finish-call echo (content + tool_calls, unkeyed raw), tool
