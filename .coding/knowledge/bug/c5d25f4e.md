Symptom: With `[general] enable_browser_inspection = true` (the user's config), Mnemo2's lead and its workers get the six on-screen `browser_*` tools in their tools array. But Mnemo2 removes WEBVIEW2_ADDITIONAL_BROWSER_ARGUMENTS at startup (src-tauri/src/mnemo2/mod.rs:584), and its Browser pane exposes no CDP endpoint, so every browser_* call can only fail.
