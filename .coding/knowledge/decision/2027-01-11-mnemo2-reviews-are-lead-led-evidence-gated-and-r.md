The user approved retaining the lead as reviewer of record while making reviews more thorough. New Mnemo2 reviews require unanchored diff inspection plus explicit intent, execution, integration, test-quality, and residual-risk evidence. High-risk changes additionally require a fresh read-only independent audit at the exact worker tip and lead adjudication; standard changes stay lead-only. Acceptance scenarios precede implementation and integrated closing checks re-verify original intent. See agent.md and src-tauri/src/mnemo2/phase3.rs.
