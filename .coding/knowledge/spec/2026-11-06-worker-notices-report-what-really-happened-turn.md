A steer ends a worker's turn at its next break with Finished and drives a follow-up turn, so pump_worker holds that Finished: the worker stays Working, turns does not change, and no notice goes out while LiveWorker.steer_pending is set. Started clears it. A Parked (nothing queued) or a 60-second absolute deadline settles it as a real finish, so a lost steer can never leave a worker Working forever. Finished, failed and blocked notices carry ` turn=<generation>.<turns> state=<state>` on their first line (notice_tag.rs), and TeamRuntime::current_notices drops any whose worker moved on, at every drain, peek and undelivered hand-off; reports, audits, plan and nudge notices are never tagged. mnemo2/src/lib/leadState.ts reads the first line and must stop at the first space. WorkerRecord.tool_errors keeps the current failing streak (last 3, 300 chars each, cleared by a success), shown in failure notices, strike nudges and team_status. team_resume on a failed worker with a live idle session makes it Idle; a delivered message clears a stale error. A nudge only names a tier whose first available model differs from the worker's. Each worker notifies the lead at cost_notice_usd ([routing] in mnemo2.toml, default 2.0, 0 off) and at each doubling, and keeps running. Tier guidance: a complete spec goes to routine or trivial even across files, unless Laya names a higher tier at about 0.75 or more.
