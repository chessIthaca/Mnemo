The Cost tab (src-tauri/src/mnemo2/insights.rs savings_report) prices each saved token once at the project's blended uncached rate. A truer value has four parts. Baseline: results were already capped at 100,000 chars (TOOL_RESULT_MAX_CHARS, 25k tokens), so credit min(raw, 25k) minus what entered; a compressed result that is then archived is one saving, not two. Duration: a token kept out would have been re-sent at the cached rate until trimmed, and old results are cut once 20 newer ones exist (turn.rs compact_old_tool_results), so about 15 requests is a fair lifetime; cached re-sends are the largest cost. Model: use the rate of the model in use. Extra requests: any lever that forces a follow-up read or expansion costs a whole re-sent prompt, about $0.034 per request here, so on glm-5.3 a saving under about 5,000 tokens loses money if it forces a follow-up. Measured 2026-11-09 at configured prices: bill about $1,087 (cached $600, uncached $322, output $165); tab total $45.53; revalued about $107 gross and $88 after expansions. Outlines' ledger is gross: 29 of 30 indexed outlines were followed by ranged reads of the file, and across 29 files outline plus ranges entered 152% of the lines a whole read would have, with 183 reads instead of 29. No row exists for old-result trimming or compaction. Spend headline and savings cover different windows: Mnemo2's ledger against the engine's lifetime stats.

Amended 2026-11-09: The Cost tab now implements this model. `savings_stats` clamps `archive` and `compression` rows to LEDGER_BASELINE_CAP_TOKENS at read time, which also corrects old rows and telescopes a compressed-then-archived result into one saving. `savings_valuation` (src/memory/mod.rs) supplies later-request counts capped at SAVED_TOKEN_LIFETIME_REQUESTS and per-session model usage; insights.rs prices entry, tail, reclaim and re-add levers from them. The card names the engine traffic it is measured against. Ledger rows now exist for `old_result_trim`, `compaction` and `outline_followup`. Replayed on this project at merge: about $85 net on $1,088 of traffic, 38.5M tokens. Outlines now start above 500 lines, so the 152% sample above describes the old 300-line rule.
