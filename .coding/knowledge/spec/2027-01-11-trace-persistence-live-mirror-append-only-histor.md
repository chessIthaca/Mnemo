SPEC: trace persistence is three files — live mirror, append-only history archive, always-on cancels log (plan e06a76d5, commits ee9a112 + 84d74ad on wt/agenticcoding; backlog 20bd12c5, F3). (1) traces.jsonl: UNCHANGED — the live ring mirror (rewrite per batch, over-cap fresh-start drops rows). (2) traces-history.jsonl: append-only, every TERMINAL record exactly once — the record is handed to the writer at its false→true is_complete transition inside with_record (under the records lock, while guaranteed still in the ring; a drain-time ring scan is LOSSY — records evicted before the drain are lost, proven by the red test). Gated on log_enabled at enqueue AND drain (full bodies are opt-in). Rotated at HISTORY_ROTATE_BYTES=64 MiB keeping the 4 newest traces-history-<unix-micros>.jsonl archives (microsecond timestamps keep consecutive rotations collision-free). (3) cancels.jsonl: always-on like provider-errors — one redacted LlmRequestSummary line per user cancel, appended at stamping time by LlmTraceLog::cancelled (both provider cancel paths — openai pump drop + anthropic complete — stamp through it); no cap (cancels are rare user actions). The archive line is the same redacted record shape the mirror writes (redacted_record_line helper, shared). Code: src/provider/trace.rs (with_record transition enqueue, writer_main cancel/history blocks, rotate_history_if_over/prune_history_archives), src-tauri/src/main.rs (startup wiring). Regression tests: cancelled_request_persists_to_cancels_log, terminal_records_archive_to_history_beyond_the_ring, history_file_rotates_and_prunes_archives.
