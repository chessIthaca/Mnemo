- 2026-11-19: no spec impact: Only dispatch-routing changes (its spec delta is attached). external-lead, tier-ladder and worker-brief requirements are unchanged: lead attachment, tiers and the worker brief behave as before; pump.rs, nudge.rs and tests.rs are touched only for strike counting and check recording.
- 2026-11-19: added step 5 (Integration check) because steps 1 and 4 ran in parallel
- 2026-11-19: Step 2 must not build the job log from run_command's `sink`. That is the live-view sink: throttled to 60 ms / 8 KiB and capped at 256 KiB per call (STREAM_CAP), so a chatty build's log would be truncated. Step 2 adds an uncapped, line-preserving log tap to run_command / pump_stream instead (for example an optional per-run log writer that receives every byte, or line, the readers read) and keeps the existing deadline loop. It does not duplicate it. The job registry holds the JoinHandle of a background task awaiting run_command with RunLimits { silence: None, total: 3 h }. Aborting it drops the future, and the TreeKillGuard then kills the tree. (Audit silenceAud1 on step 1, 2026-11-19.)
