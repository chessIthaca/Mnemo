- 2026-11-22: Decision 8's classifier is amended deliberately after audit usageAudit01 found that bare keywords over-match. is_usage_limit is true when the text contains 'usage limit', 'limit exhausted', 'insufficient_quota' or 'exceeded your current'; or when it contains 'quota', 'credit' or 'billing' together with one of '429', '402', 'limit', 'exceeded', 'exhausted' or 'insufficient'. This covers GLM's 'Weekly/Monthly Limit Exhausted' and OpenRouter-style 402 'Insufficient credits', and excludes unrelated mentions of a credit or billing field.
