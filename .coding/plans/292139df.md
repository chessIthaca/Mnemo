# Plan: Make Mnemo2 reviews intent-driven and independently auditable

## Goal
Keep the lead as reviewer of record while requiring substantive intent/evidence review and adding fresh, read-only independent audits for high-risk changes.

## Kind
implementation

## Context
The user values the lead's implementation context but finds current reviews insufficiently thorough. Preserve exact-tip checks and finding gates, but review more than worker summaries and test counts. Decisions already taken: (1) every lead review records an unanchored diff pass plus intent, execution-path, integration, test-quality, and residual-risk assessments; (2) standard changes remain lead-only, while the lead marks shared contracts, persistence, concurrency, security, large or cross-cutting work review_risk=high; (3) high-risk merges require a fresh independent reviewer worker at the exact target tip and a lead adjudication of that audit; (4) reviewer workers are read-only, do not commit, do not tick plan steps, and receive plan/intent plus target diff rather than the implementation worker's conclusions; (5) the lead remains reviewer of record, and closing integration must re-verify the original user scenarios. New high-risk plans must include a `## Acceptance scenarios` section with observable bullets; all other plans are encouraged to include it. Legacy ledgers deserialize as implementation/standard with no evidence and require a fresh review before merge. Do not require different models for independence—fresh context is required, a different model is preferred when available. Verification: cargo test --workspace and mnemo2 npm test/npm run check. Preserve unrelated uncommitted work and warning-free builds.

\## Acceptance scenarios
- A standard worker cannot merge with only a summary and passing tests: the lead records unanchored-pass, intent, execution, integration, test-quality, and residual-risk evidence.
- A high-risk worker cannot merge without a completed read-only audit linked to the same worker tip and a nonempty lead adjudication.
- A fix commit invalidates the prior lead review and makes the previous audit tip stale.
- An audit worker cannot mutate files, commit, merge, spawn work, ask the user, or tick a plan step; its completion notice asks the lead to adjudicate the target review, not merge the reviewer.
- Audit dispatch supplies the plan/intent and target branch/tip without the implementation worker's review summary.
- The Mnemo2 detail/review UI exposes risk, structured evidence, and audit linkage while remaining compatible with old records.
- Closing review records retain the structured evidence, and the lead workflow requires an integrated check of the original acceptance scenarios.
