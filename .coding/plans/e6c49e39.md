# Plan: Compact, pinned worker header in mission control

## Goal
Mission control's worker detail opens with a compact header pinned to the top: a modest title line with aligned badges, a brief that clearly expands and collapses in place, clickable workflow steps, a small professional action toolbar and tidy section tabs. The brief no longer repeats as a "LEAD · dispatched" bubble in the thread.

## Kind
implementation

## Context
WHY: the user likes the worker detail header (mnemo2/src/components/WorkerDetail.svelte) but asked for a compact redesign pinned to the top. Their feedback, quoted, with what each point means:
- "it should be pinned to the top": the header stays visible while the thread/review/timeline below scrolls.
- "the headline is too big": make the worker name a compact title (roughly 15-17px).
- "the lines are not aligned (look at glm5.3)": status pill, ModelBadge (dot + model text) and tier/effort pills must sit on one row, same height, vertically centred.
- "the expand is good but should allow me to collapse the same place too and it doesn't really show any reason to believe that there is more": Show more / Show less in the same spot; a visible overflow cue (fade or ellipsis) when clamped; no toggle when nothing is hidden.
- "I also can't click on the brief, implement, verify, review, merge": make the workflow steps buttons. Brief expands the brief, Implement opens the Thread tab, Verify and Review open the Review tab, Merge opens the Review tab at the merge panel. Keep current/done/blocked styling and aria-current.
- "the buttons on the bottom are too big a font and look unprofessional but we do want them": restyle the Thread/Review/Timeline tabs and the branch line compactly.
- "I don't know why I need the lead thingy on the bottom right": the brief repeated as the thread's first lead bubble; drop it, the header carries the brief.
- The right-hand column of large action buttons (Diff, Steer, Compact, Pause/Resume, Stop, Delete) becomes a compact toolbar in the pinned header; every action, its disabled logic and aria-label stays.

## Acceptance scenarios
- In mission control, scrolling a long worker thread keeps the worker header (name, status, model, workflow steps, actions, tabs) visible at the top.
- The worker name is a compact title; status, model and tier chips sit on one line at the same height.
- A long brief shows a fade or ellipsis with 'Show more'; clicking it expands in place and the same spot then offers 'Show less'. A short brief shows no toggle.
- Clicking Implement opens the Thread tab, Verify/Review/Merge open the Review tab, Brief expands the brief.
- Thread/Review/Timeline tabs and the branch line are styled compactly; the thread no longer starts with a 'LEAD · dispatched' brief bubble.
- npm --prefix mnemo2 run check and npm --prefix mnemo2 test pass.
