### R2-1 (LOW) — `cardMarkup` cannot bound the LAST testid card; the per-day epoch-day pin is timezone-dependent
**Where**: frontend/src/components/views/DashboardView.test.tsx:100-107 (`cardMarkup`, the `next === -1` → end-of-string fallback) and :170-178 (the per-day test); frontend/src/components/views/DashboardView.tsx:208 (`dashboard-per-day` is the last `data-testid` in the view — the recent-events card at :235 and the prompt-cache card at :260 carry none).

**Issue**: the helper's contract ("bounded by the next card's marker", :96-98) silently fails for the last testid card: the rendered marker sequence is `dashboard-view` → `dashboard-per-kind` → `dashboard-per-day` → (no more), so `cardMarkup(render(), "dashboard-per-day")` slices from the per-day card's opening div to END OF STRING, absorbing the recent-events and prompt-cache cards. Both per-day assertions can then be satisfied by the recent-events row instead of the per-day card:
- `toContain(fmtTokens(12_000))` — the recent-events fixture's `tokens_before` is ALSO 12_000 (test.tsx:72, rendered at DashboardView.tsx:249), so this assertion cannot discriminate the per-day figure at all: scrambling the per-day tokens column would not fail it, on any machine.
- `toContain(new Date(19_675 * 86_400_000).toLocaleDateString())` — the recent row renders `fmtTime(1_700_000_000)` = `new Date(1.7e12).toLocaleString()` (:42-44, :243). The fixture bucket (2023-11-14T00:00Z) and the fixture event (2023-11-14T22:13:20Z) land on the SAME local calendar date on a UTC machine (and a CET machine), and in those locales `toLocaleString` starts with the `toLocaleDateString` form — so the expected "11/14/2023" is a substring of the recent row's "11/14/2023, 10:13:20 PM".

**Concrete failure scenario** (exactly the refactor L2 was written to catch): on a UTC or CET machine, a future "consistency" refactor copies StatsView's `× 1000` into `fmtDay` → every per-day label in the shipped app renders Jan-1-1970 (the ~86× shift reaches users) → this test still PASSES, because the date assertion is satisfied by the recent-events row inside the leaked slice (and the figure assertion by the fixture collision); `npx vitest run` stays green. On a UTC−5 machine the test WOULD catch it (the bucket's local date Nov 13 ≠ the event's Nov 14, so the expected string appears nowhere in the slice) — the pin's strength depends on the machine's timezone, and the test's own comment ("Copying StatsView's day-start-seconds × 1000 convention here would shift every label by ~86× and fail exactly this assertion", :171-174) overstates what it guarantees. Dropping the per-day card entirely IS still caught (the marker check fails); only regressions INSIDE the card leak. The per-kind use of `cardMarkup` is sound — `dashboard-per-kind` has a next marker; the hole is specific to the last testid card.

**Fix** (one line in the view, matching the per-kind pattern): give the recent-events card its own marker — `data-testid="dashboard-recent"` on the div at DashboardView.tsx:235. The per-day slice then terminates at it, both assertions become genuinely card-scoped, no test change is needed (the helper already looks for the next marker), and `cardMarkup`'s doc comment becomes true for every use. (Test-only alternative, no view change: pin the position — `expect(card.indexOf(expectedDate)).toBeLessThan(card.indexOf("recent events"))` — and make the per-day fixture's `saved_tokens` unique, e.g. 12_345, so it cannot collide with the recent row's before-size.)
