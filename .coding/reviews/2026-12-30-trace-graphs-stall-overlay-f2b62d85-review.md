`const allHidden = PHASE_SEGMENTS.every((s) => hidden.has(s.key));` iterates all 9 segments including stall, but `hasData` is computed over the stall-free height visibility. Hide all 8 stacked phases while leaving `stall` visible → `allHidden` is false, `hasData` is false, and the chart shows "No phase timings recorded yet." — factually wrong (timings exist; they're hidden). The mirrored TokenChart gets this right: `const allHidden = stacked.every((s) => hidden.has(s.key));` (line 394, over the cached-free stack). Before this change the two forms were equivalent (stall was stacked), so the edge is introduced here. Fix: compute `allHidden` over `stacked` (hoist the `stacked` definition above line 278, or inline the filter). Cosmetic — no data or height impact — but it diverges from the precedent this change deliberately mirrors.
