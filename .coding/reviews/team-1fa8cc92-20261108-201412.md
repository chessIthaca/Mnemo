In file_edit/multi_edit ops, a read-receipt cite {from_read, lines, new_string} splits new_string on \n and the trailing empty string is KEPT: new_string ending "\n" yields ONE extra blank line, ending "\n\n" yields TWO; and a cite replaced with new_string "" does NOT delete the range — it collapses it to exactly ONE blank line (verified 2026-11-08 in plan 1fa8cc92: every `mnemo1_enforce` field removal via cite+"" left a stray blank inside the object/interface, and a test-insert cite ending "\n\n" doubled the separator blank; the Rust side behaved the same).
