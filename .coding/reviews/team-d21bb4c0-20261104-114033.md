### BUG: Orphaned laya-serve trees held the Laya checkpoint in RAM after Mnemo was force-ended

Symptom: eight orphaned laya-serve trees (laya-serve.exe, then python.exe, then python.exe), about 17 GB of RAM, left by Mnemo2 processes that had been force-ended or had crashed. Root causes: LayaManager killed only the laya-serve.exe root (the python descendants survived even an orderly stop), and a force-killed host never ran its stop or Drop, while Windows does not end children with their parent. Fix (plan d21bb4c0, merge 191edd92): src/process_tree.rs KillOnCloseJob (a Windows Job Object with JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_KILL_ON_JOB_CLOSE, shared with the shell tool's TreeKillGuard). LayaManager assigns laya-serve to a job at spawn and kill_tree kills the job, then the root; on Unix it uses a process group. Because the job handle lives in the host, a crash or force-kill also ends the tree. Regression: stopping_the_manager_kills_the_server_tree.
