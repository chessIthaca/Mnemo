## Plan notes (retired at close)

### DECISION: delta reads and signature skeletons were removed; outlines stay and the quality grade is fill-only

Decided 2026-11-09 (plan e555e701, worker dropDeltaSkeleton01). read_files no longer serves a signature skeleton for an unchanged whole re-read or a diff for a changed one. Every whole re-read serves the file again; only a source file over 500 lines (OUTLINE_MIN_LINES) is served as its signature outline, on every whole read, with outline_followup charging later ranged reads.

Why: the real savings ledger showed both levers not worth their code. In six weeks, skeleton had 139 events and 132,619 tokens (about $0.31), and delta_read had 3 events and 3,096 tokens. Together that was about 0.03% of traffic.

What went with them:
- ReadCache's served-content baseline (snapshot, record, forget_served, diff_if_small);
- the Tool::on_context_reduced and AgentLoop::note_context_reduced reset;
- the quality grade's waste and stale-read signals, which only skeletons fed. QualityReport is now grade, fill_pct and decision_density, and score = 100 - fill_penalty. The Mnemo1 context popup lost its waste and stale-reads rows.

The persisted key [general.optimizer] delta_reads, optimizer_delta_reads and the Mnemo2 wire field keep their names so configs load; the key now gates only outlines and is labelled "Outline reads". Old delta_read and skeleton ledger rows stay as history; the Cost tab labels them "Retired." Don't re-add a re-read cache without ledger evidence that re-reads cost real money.

Re-store any durable fact above without plan/branch references.
